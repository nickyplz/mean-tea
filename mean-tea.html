5,000 years of chaos.

Tea was discovered in 2737 BC when Chinese emperor Shen Nong had some leaves blow into his boiling water and decided that was fine. He was right. It has been fine ever since.

For most of its history, tea was currency, medicine, and the reason empires went to war. The British addiction to it was so severe they started growing opium in India to trade for Chinese tea — which did not go well for anyone. This is not that tea.

Irish breakfast tea — the base for Morning Mayhem — emerged from the British colonial tea trade and was blended strong to cut through the calcium in Irish water. It has always been aggressive. We kept that.

Sencha — the base for Green Static — is the most consumed tea in Japan, produced by steaming fresh leaves immediately after harvest to stop oxidation. Sharp and grassy. The exact opposite of being chill about things.
